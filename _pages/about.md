I am a PhD candidate in the School of Communication at Hong Kong Baptist University and currently a visiting scholar at The Pennsylvania State University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of human–AI communication, health communication, science communication, and AI for social good. I am particularly interested in understanding how emerging technologies shape communication processes, individual perceptions, and behavioral responses.

Methodologically, I primarily employ quantitative approaches, particularly surveys and experiments, to investigate communication phenomena and their implications for individuals and society.
